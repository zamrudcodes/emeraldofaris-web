# B7P Brand Profile

## Basic Info
- **Brand:** Bakso Tujuh Pemuda (B7P)
- **Kategori:** F&B - Bakso / Indonesian Street Food
- **Lokasi:** Jl. Pengadilan No.51, Bogor (tengah kota)
- **Status:** Beroperasi, 1 cabang (Bogor)
- **Google Maps Rating:** 4.7★ / 3135+ reviews
- **Halal:** MUI Certified (32110035791751225)

## Produk
### Core Menu
- Bakso campur (daging urat, daging cincang) — start 16K
- Tetelan goreng (signature differentiator)
- Tahu Bakso / "Tabso" (menu baru)
- Mie ayam homemade "bumbu lekoh"
- Es teh Solo

### Price Range
- Entry: Rp 16,000
- Signature (dengan tetelan): ~Rp 39,000
- Positioning: affordable premium street food

## Operasional
- **Hours:** Senin-Jumat 09.00-21.00 | Sabtu-Minggu 09.00-22.00
- **Model:** Dine-in + takeaway (street vendor / kaki lima upgraded)
- **Peak hours:** Malam & weekend (lebih rame)

## Brand Perception
### Strengths
1. **Tetelan goreng** = moat. Disebut di hampir semua review.
2. **Kualitas daging** = padat, bukan tepung. Called out berkali-kali.
3. **Kuah panas** = emotional hook ("garansi kuah panas")
4. **Halal certified** = trust signal kuat
5. **Harga terjangkau** = mass market friendly
6. **Rating 4.7★** = social proof massive

### Potential Weaknesses
- Hanya 1 lokasi
- Positioning "kaki lima" mungkin batasi aspirational segment
- Ketergantungan pada 1 signature item (tetelan)
- Harga Rp 39K bisa jadi resistance buat sebagian segmen
- Jam operasional gak sampe late-night (tutup jam 9-10 malam)

## Kompetitor (Bogor)
- Bakso Ujo (Jl. Re. Abdullah)
- Various bakso malang / bakso mercon di area Bogor
- B7P differentiator utama: tetelan goreng + rating tinggi
